# Ashbound v0.4 — Expedition Hub, Resources & Meta Progression

v0.4 adds the first persistent expedition loop without replacing v0.3 combat. The profile increases options, information, rerolls, recovery, and probability control. Run weapons, elements, skills, armor, sets, relics, and corruption remain the dominant source of power and reset with each expedition. Progression pacing is not considered balanced until repeated human solo and local multiplayer sessions are complete.

## Root architecture and save profile

`MetaProgressionProfile` is a versioned plain-data model with a profile ID independent of `P1`–`P4`. It contains persistent currencies, facility state, weapon/skill/relic/armor/set unlock pools, expedition upgrades, lore, defeated bosses, selected preparation, and lifetime statistics. It does not contain transient health, position, rooms, run gear, relic inventory, or corruption state.

`MetaProgressionStore` owns JSON load/save under `Application.persistentDataPath/Profile/meta-profile.json`. Saves use a temporary file and replacement. Missing data produces defaults. Older valid schemas normalize to schema 2. Invalid files are moved aside and replaced with safe defaults. Current local play uses one host profile; the boundary is suitable for later per-account stores without coupling identity to Player 1 input.

`RunResourceInventory` is represented by the service's separate `RunResources` wallet. Encounter and salvage rewards enter this wallet visibly during the expedition. `ResolveRun` is the only normal transfer into persistent currency. Abandon/reset retains zero, which prevents reset farming.

## Resource economy and retention

| Resource | Prototype sources | Main uses |
|---|---|---|
| Ash | Normal encounters, salvage, bosses | Basic facilities and research |
| Ember Shards | Elite/hard encounters, Rare+ salvage, bosses | Forge, research, higher upgrades |
| Ancient Alloy | Mini-boss/boss, Epic/Legendary salvage | Advanced and Legendary research |
| Corruption Fragments | Final boss milestone | Late Forge and future corruption research |

Normal failure retains 70% Ash, 50% Ember, 25% Alloy, and 0% Corruption Fragments. A reached boss milestone adds 15 percentage points. Recovery Training adds 3 percentage points, while upgrade contribution is capped at 15 points. Completion retains 100%. Values live in `ProgressionTuningDefinition` rather than combat controllers. Deep encounters and bosses supply the rare currencies, so intentional early death is a poor farming strategy.

Salvage rises with rarity: Common gives small Ash; Advanced gives more Ash; Rare adds Ember; Epic adds Alloy; Legendary adds larger rare-material amounts. The economy invariant makes the modeled merchant price strictly more expensive in Ash and never lower in other materials than salvage value, preventing an obvious buy/dismantle loop.

## Hub facilities and every authored upgrade

The existing MainMenu scene functions as the primitive Hub and local-party lobby. `FacilityLevelChanged` and `ProfileChanged` events are hooks for future station models, shelves, props, and animation.

### Expedition Table

1. Survey Ledger — records highest region/encounter.
2. Route Annotations — +1 future route reveal.
3. Boss Ledger — displays defeated bosses and milestone information.
4. Deep Chart — another future route reveal.

### Forge

1. Weapon Research — unlocks five primary elemental weapons and Winterglass armor into reward pools.
2. Elemental Forging — unlocks five alternate combinations and Stormcaller armor.
3. Weapon Skill Research — enables ten researched skills and Venomblood/Riftwalker armor.
4. Rarity Craftsmanship — +5% capped Rare-or-higher weighting.
5. Legendary Research — after Expedition Table level 2, unlocks Moon-Eater, Hearth-Sunder, and their skills into eligible pools. They are never permanent starting items.

### Quartermaster

1. Merchant Stock I — +1 future merchant option.
2. Merchant Network — +5% future merchant-node weighting.
3. Negotiation — first future merchant reroll discount hook.
4. Salvage Training — +10% salvage yield.
5. Supply Cache — start with 8 run Ash.

### Infirmary

1. Field Medicine — +10% checkpoint/future Rest healing.
2. Recovery Training — +3 percentage points failure retention.
3. Emergency Supply — extra future Rest recovery-option hook.
4. Vitality Training I — +2% base maximum HP.
5. Vitality Training II — another +2%.
6. Vitality Training III — another +2%.

Authored permanent maximum-health gain is exactly 6%. The tuning hard cap is 8%, and `Combatant` has a final 10% defensive clamp. There is no permanent damage, critical, or PvP multiplier. Rare weighting is capped at 15%; elemental preparation bias is capped at 20%.

### Research Station

1. Scavenging — +5% Rare weighting and foundational elemental relic unlocks.
2. Cartography — +1 future route reveal.
3. Relic Analysis — one reroll at each relic checkpoint plus advanced synergy relic unlocks.
4. Equipment Appraisal — richer comparison-information hook.
5. Elemental Affinity — enables capped elemental preparation research.

### Archive

1. Recovered Shelves — discovered note display.
2. Insignia Index — expanded optional record capacity.
3. Boss Observations — defeated-boss records.
4. Legendary Folio — Legendary research/lore display hook.

Archive use is optional. The placeholder entries do not explain corruption, the expedition cycle, final PvP, or every boss identity.

## Preparation system

The host selects exactly one preparation before launch. Hunter's Preparation adds a 5% Rare weighting bias. Frost Research adds a 15% Frost weighting bias without guaranteeing Frost. Cartographer's Notes adds one route-reveal hook. Merchant Contract reserves a free-first-reroll hook. Field Supplies adds 15% checkpoint/future Rest recovery. Facility-level prerequisites control availability, and the selected preparation is copied into runtime state at launch.

## Equipment rewards, salvage, and unlock pools

After each relic checkpoint, `EquipmentRewardDraft` queues every player separately. It offers an eligible weapon and armor item from persistent unlock pools. Cards show rarity, category, element, family/slot, skill, current equipment, and salvage return. The owning player can equip, dismantle into run resources, or leave. One player's choice never changes another player's gear.

Unlocked means an asset may enter a weighted pool. It does not grant an infinite owned copy or starting equipment. Locked skills are removed from a runtime weapon copy until Weapon Skill Research unlocks them. Depth, risk hooks, modest rarity research, and the selected elemental preparation affect weights; none guarantees a completed build.

The future expedition schema exposes five major regions plus a Final Area, 8–10 nodes per region, a 30–45 minute experienced target, all ten requested node types, reward-quality ranges, and Rest/Temper definitions. It does not generate 50 rooms in v0.4.

## Telemetry and debug testing

Local telemetry schema 3 adds resources collected/retained/lost, Hub materials spent, facility levels, active preparation, equipment acquired/dismantled, rerolls, highest region, defeated bosses, and completion status. It remains local.

Press F1 in the Hub or a run and open **Meta page**. Controls can add each currency, zero wallets, set/max facilities, unlock all gear, reset the profile, force preparations, simulate failure/success, select forced reward rarity, start an equipment reward, and display the save path. The Equipment page retains v0.3 weapon/set/build controls.

Manual loop: add resources, upgrade Forge/Infirmary/Research, choose one preparation at the Expedition Table, launch, clear an encounter, take a relic, equip or dismantle gear, inspect run resources, then fail or complete. Return to the Hub and confirm retained totals, levels, unlocks, statistics, and archive entries. Restart the application and confirm the same host profile loads.

## Risks and recommended v0.5

Remaining balance risks include salvage yield versus encounter duration, failure retention feeling too generous or punitive, rarity bias stacking, players ignoring unskilled Rare weapons, checkpoint reward cadence, and local groups disagreeing about host-profile spending. The merchant price invariant blocks the direct modeled buy/salvage profit loop, but an actual merchant economy still needs end-to-end testing. Human sessions must determine pacing.

Recommended v0.5: build the first data-driven region route graph with Normal/Hard/Elite, Merchant, Event, Rest, Treasure, and Boss nodes; implement the actual merchant and Rest/Temper screens; and use telemetry to tune a 30–45 minute route. Keep online transport, cloud profiles, final Hub art, and major content expansion separate.

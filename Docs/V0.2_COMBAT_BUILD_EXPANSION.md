# Ashbound v0.2 — combat and build expansion

## Architecture changes

The existing composition and authority model remains intact. `CombatService` is still the only damage authority, `RunManager` still owns progression, and weapons, relics, rooms, bosses, and corruption remain data-driven.

New extension points:

- `CombatFeedback` listens to resolved damage and translates `ImpactTier` and `DamageElement` into configurable hit stop, camera shake, sparks, hit flash, and audio hooks.
- `WeaponDefinition` now describes family, mechanic, movement commitment, crit adjustment, projectile behavior, combo threshold, tags, and mechanic power.
- `DamageInfo` carries element, impact tier, and per-attack crit bonus without moving combat rules into player code.
- `StatusEffectController` supports Chill/Freeze, boss Frost vulnerability, Poison, Void Mark, and Corrosion in addition to the original effects.
- `UpgradeEffectController` interprets reusable dash, threshold, delayed, heavy, combo, status, and death procs. Secondary damage cannot recurse.
- `BuildAnalyzer` ranks all ten v0.2 build themes and still gives reflections at most four representative relics.
- The expedition contains six reward encounters, including an elite check and Mini-Boss, then the Cinder Regent and unchanged corruption gate.
- Telemetry schema 2 records weapons, tag counts, damage categories/elements, crit/status damage, proc counts, boss timings, and final-side result locally.

## Weapon roster

| Family | Prototype weapon | Mechanical identity |
|---|---|---|
| Sword | Wayfarer's Edge | Balanced wide melee baseline |
| Spear | Long Reach | Narrow long thrust; repeated hits on one target gain damage |
| Greatsword | Bell Cleaver | Slow, wide, high knockback heavy impacts |
| Katana | Moon Shear | Fast directional melee; forgiving post-dash crit window |
| Dual Blades | Twin Embers | Short reach; combo steadily accelerates attacks |
| Bow | Vault Bow | Long projectile; every third shot is a charged heavy shot |
| Staff | Ashen Staff | Ranged spell; every fourth projectile is Arcane-charged and adopts a dominant element |
| Spellblade | Rift Brand | Melee rhythm; every fourth strike releases a short-range magic wave |

Corrupted players and solo reflections keep their selected weapon. Weapon choice changes range, commitment, coverage, proc frequency, and timing rather than health or class role.

## Reflection analysis

Each owned relic counts once for each distinct tag. `BuildAnalyzer` filters to Fire, Frost, Lightning, Poison, Void, Critical, Bleed, Heavy, Combo, and DashPrecision, sorts by count then stable enum order, and selects the top two. Each selected theme maps to two representative relics, capped at four total. This keeps the reflection recognizable without cloning arbitrary proc state.

## Testing weapons and builds

1. Open `Assets/Scenes/MainMenu.unity`, enter a run, and press F1.
2. Select a player and click any of the eight weapon-family buttons.
3. Use **Grant by tag** for a quick direction, or page through **Specific relics** for exact prerequisites/capstones.
4. Use **Jump Mini-Boss** to compare a partially formed build and **Jump Final Boss** to test corruption/reflection.
5. Toggle hit stop, camera shake, and VFX independently to isolate feedback.
6. Use Reset build before changing test configurations. Debugged runs remain marked in telemetry.

## Known balance risks

- The 20–25 minute target is represented by six 145–180 second encounter budgets, but requires human timing validation.
- Greatsword hit stop and Dual Blades proc frequency may be too strong in final PvP.
- Ranged safety, Bow charged cadence, and Staff elemental adoption need controller playtests.
- Poison/Bleed tick scaling and delayed Void echoes can produce bursty multi-proc moments.
- Mini-Boss health and four-player scaling are initial tuning values, not proof of balance.
- Hit stop uses global simulation time in this local prototype; a future networked version must use presentation-only feedback.

## Human playtest checklist

- Complete solo runs with all eight weapons; record room and boss times.
- Compare melee/ranged safety, attack commitment, hit readability, and control feel.
- Force each elemental and physical theme, then at least eight two-theme combinations.
- Verify normal enemies freeze, the Mini-Boss/boss only gain Frost vulnerability, and corrosion reduces outgoing damage.
- Complete 2–4 player runs with physical controllers; verify co-op protection and final-team damage.
- Confirm corrupted players/reflections retain weapon/build identity and VFX remain readable.
- Inspect telemetry JSON after normal and debug runs; keep those samples separate.

## Recommended v0.3

Use human telemetry to tune weapon risk/reward, encounter pacing, build pick rates, boss time-to-kill, reflection win rate, and corrupted-team win rate. Then add controller-only lobby/relic navigation and a minimal host-authoritative LAN transport behind the existing input and combat seams. Do not expand content until the vertical slice is fair and readable.

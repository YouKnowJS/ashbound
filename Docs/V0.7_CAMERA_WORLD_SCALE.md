# v0.7 Camera and world scale

Ashbound now uses a moving orthographic camera throughout combat, services, and playable transition paths. The camera keeps the established top-down three-quarter angle while its focus and zoom move independently with configurable damping.

## Framing behavior

- Solo mode follows the living player with a short movement-direction anticipation offset.
- Automatic multiplayer mode follows the centroid of all living local players.
- Party spread increases orthographic size until the space-specific maximum is reached. Regrouping returns toward the minimum.
- A space-specific soft spread limit raises `ArenaCamera.SoftSpreadLimitExceeded`. This is the future regroup/tether integration point; it does not move or teleport players.
- Players beyond the readable viewport receive color-coded edge indicators.
- Boss framing includes the boss, and final PvP includes the solo reflection when present.
- Combat, Large, Elite, Boss, Transition, Service, and Final PvP contexts apply small data-driven framing offsets.
- The focus is clamped to scaled technical bounds. Non-playable world aprons and silhouettes extend beyond those bounds so the camera does not reveal an empty void.

## Effective combat-space sizes

| Space | Category | Scale | Effective technical bounds | Zoom range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Crooked Threshold | Small | 1.12 | 26.9 × 26.9 | 11.2–15.0 |
| Twin Courts | Medium | 1.45 | 46.4 × 36.3 | 12.2–18.5 |
| Branching Ruins | Medium | 1.50 | 51.0 × 40.5 | 12.5–19.5 |
| Broken Ring | Large | 1.85 | 70.3 × 57.4 | 14.5–23.5 |
| Divided Hall | Large | 1.90 | 70.3 × 58.9 | 14.8–24.0 |

Medium is now the default Normal/Hard combat size. Large is used by Elite, Challenge, regional Boss, and final-area gameplay. Small is reserved for Treasure, Relic, Merchant, Rest, and Event pockets. Existing irregular boundaries, connected courtyards, branching lanes, halls, bridges, crossings, and transition corridors remain authored as replaceable data rather than a square-room assumption.

`RoomView` scales playable sections, paths, entrances, exits, boundaries, spawn points, and obstacle placement at runtime. Obstacle footprints scale more slowly than the battlefield to create useful separation and firing lanes. Each space also creates a non-colliding world apron, distant silhouettes, landmark hooks, and prefab replacement hooks for later cliffs, ruins, towers, forests, architecture, and skyline art.

## Debug controls

Open **F1 → Camera** to:

- enable or disable follow;
- force Solo, Party Centroid, or Automatic follow;
- force minimum, maximum, or automatic zoom;
- display the party centroid, spread, and camera clamp bounds;
- snap to current targets;
- load an enlarged Medium or Large space;
- inspect context, focus, centroid, spread/soft limit, zoom range, clamp rectangle, edge indicators, and regroup-hook state.

## Verification

Edit Mode validates scale ratios, Medium/Large minimum dimensions, camera tuning ranges, world depth, and node-to-space categories. Play Mode moves a solo player during combat and transition to prove damped follow and clamp behavior, then separates and regroups a two-player party to prove centroid framing, spread zoom, maximum zoom, and the soft regroup hook.
